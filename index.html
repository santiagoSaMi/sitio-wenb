About me

Engineering, in and out of class

I'm a Colombian student who moves comfortably between coursework and side projects.

I study Computer Engineering at Universidad de La Sabana, in Colombia, with coursework that also stretches into global business and comparative management. Outside the classroom I'm usually the one building the tool the group project actually needs.

In 2026 I went on exchange to Saitama University in Japan through the STEPS program, supported by a JASSO scholarship. Living there sharpened my interest in how process, discipline, and technology come together — something I now bring back into every project I build, from desktop apps to cloud architectures.

When I'm not studying or coding, you'll find me following volleyball, playing video games (Nintendo and Xbox both have a place in my heart), or exploring anything to do with Japanese language and culture.
